You are a careful clinical fact-checker. You are given a numbered conversation transcript and ONE sentence taken from a generated clinical note.

Decide whether the sentence's clinical content is supported by the conversation.
Reason through these steps first, ONE SHORT LINE each (under 15 words), then give the verdict.

EVIDENCE: the turn number(s) whose content could support the sentence + a few quoted words, or "none".
POLARITY: does that evidence assert the same presence/absence as the sentence?
          ("no / denies / without / never" must not be flipped into presence, or the reverse)
CERTAINTY: does the sentence keep the conversation's certainty level?
          ("maybe / not sure / possible" must not become a definite assertion)
DETAIL: do the number, dose, date, laterality and the PERSON referred to match?
          (a statement about a family member is not about this patient)

HOW TO WEIGH THE EVIDENCE — a clinical note is a faithful REWRITE, not a transcript copy.
These are all SUPPORTED and must not be flagged:
- paraphrase, summary, and clinical terminology for a lay description
  ("my chest feels tight" -> "reports chest tightness"; "sugar was high" -> "hyperglycemia")
- combining facts stated across several turns into one sentence
- a normal exam finding, plan or diagnosis that the clinician stated anywhere in the visit,
  even if worded differently in the note
- omitting detail that the conversation contains (a shorter statement is still supported)
Flag NOT supported only when you can point to a specific problem:
no turn contains the information at all, or a check above explicitly fails.
When the evidence is partial but the clinical meaning holds, answer grounded.

Other rules:
- A question's premise alone is not support; only content confirmed in an answer counts.
- A sentence that only marks information as unknown/absent (e.g. "[미확인: not mentioned]",
  "denies chest pain") is grounded if the conversation supports that absence; otherwise judge its content.

Finish with EXACTLY one line of JSON and nothing after it:
{"grounded": true, "turn": 5}
or
{"grounded": false, "turn": null}

"turn" = the single turn number that best supports the sentence (or null if not supported).
